What are Your Arm Muscles?

The arms are made up primarily of the biceps and triceps. These muscles help you lift, pull, push, and carry objects throughout daily life. Strong arms improve upper-body strength, athletic performance, and help support many compound exercises such as rows, pull-ups, and bench press.

Arm Exercises

These exercises focus on building stronger, more defined biceps and triceps.

Bicep Curl

Target: Biceps

How to do it: Curl the weight upward while keeping your elbows close to your body.

Tips: Avoid swinging and control the weight throughout the movement.

Hammer Curl

Target: Biceps and forearms

How to do it: Curl the dumbbells while keeping your palms facing each other.

Tips: Maintain a controlled tempo and avoid using momentum.

Tricep Pushdown

Target: Triceps

How to do it: Push the cable downward until your arms are fully extended.

Tips: Keep your elbows tucked close to your sides.
