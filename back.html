What is Your Back?

The back is made up of several important muscles including the latissimus dorsi, trapezius, rhomboids, and erector spinae. These muscles help you maintain good posture, support the spine, and perform pulling movements. Training your back improves overall strength, stability, and athletic performance while reducing the risk of injury.

Back Exercises

These exercises target different areas of the back to help build strength, stability, and better posture.

Lat Pulldown

Target: Latissimus dorsi (lats)

How to do it: Pull the bar down toward your upper chest while keeping your back straight.

Tips: Avoid swinging your body and focus on pulling with your back muscles.

Seated Row

Target: Middle back

How to do it: Pull the handle toward your stomach while squeezing your shoulder blades together.

Tips: Keep your chest up and avoid rounding your shoulders.

Face Pull

Target: Upper back and rear delts

How to do it: Pull the rope attachment toward your face while keeping elbows high.

Tips: Focus on controlled movement and squeezing the upper back.
