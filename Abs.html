What are your ABS?

The abdominal muscles are a key part of your core, helping stabilize your body during movement, protect your spine, and support posture. Your abs include the rectus abdominis, obliques, and transverse abdominis — all working together to provide strength, balance, and control. Training your abs improves stability, reduces injury risk, and enhances performance in nearly every exercise.

Core Exercises

Follow these exercises to strengthen your core and improve stability.

Crunches

Target: Upper abs

How to do it: Lift your shoulders off the ground and squeeze your core.

Tips: Avoid pulling your neck forward.

Plank

Target: Entire core

How to do it: Hold a straight body position on elbows and toes.

Tips: Keep hips level and avoid sagging.

Leg Raises

Target: Lower abs

How to do it: Lift your legs upward while keeping them straight.

Tips: Keep your lower back pressed into the floor.
