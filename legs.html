What are Your Legs?

The legs are made up of several major muscle groups including the quadriceps, hamstrings, glutes, and calves. Strong legs improve athletic performance, support everyday movement, enhance balance, and provide the foundation for overall strength and stability.

Leg Exercises

These exercises target the major muscles of the legs to help improve strength, endurance, and lower-body power.

Squat

Target: Quadriceps, glutes, hamstrings

How to do it: Lower your body by bending your knees and hips, then return to standing.

Tips: Keep your chest up and knees in line with your toes.

Leg Press

Target: Quadriceps and glutes

How to do it: Push the platform away from you using your legs until almost straight.

Tips: Do not lock your knees at the top of the movement.

Calf Raise

Target: Calves

How to do it: Raise your heels off the ground and slowly lower them back down.

Tips: Perform the movement slowly for better muscle engagement.
