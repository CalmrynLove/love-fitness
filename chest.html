What is Your Chest?

The chest is mainly made up of the pectoral muscles, which play an important role in pushing movements. Strong chest muscles improve upper-body strength, support shoulder stability, and help with exercises such as push-ups, bench presses, and chest flys.

Chest Exercises

These exercises target the chest muscles to help build strength, muscle size, and pushing power.

Bench Press

Target: Chest, shoulders, and triceps

How to do it: Press the bar upward from your chest until your arms are extended.

Tips: Keep your feet firmly planted and avoid bouncing the bar.

Push-Up

Target: Chest, shoulders, and triceps

How to do it: Lower your body toward the floor and push back up.

Tips: Keep your body in a straight line throughout the movement.

Chest Fly

Target: Chest

How to do it: Bring the weights together in an arc motion above your chest.

Tips: Maintain a slight bend in your elbows throughout the exercise.
